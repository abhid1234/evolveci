Failure evidenceStage, reason, retryability, and raw logs.

Alpha boundaries
EvolveCI

Useful precisely because the claim is narrow.

What it can show

A chosen follow-up became measurably different between two revisions.

With the task, verifier, patch, and raw logs attached.

What it cannot show

That it predicted your roadmap or measured maintainability in general.

Worktrees are checkout isolation, not a security sandbox.

Built-in today: Codex adapter.

The adapter contract is vendor-neutral; other harnesses are future work, not launch claims.

Early, executable, and open for criticism.evolveci
